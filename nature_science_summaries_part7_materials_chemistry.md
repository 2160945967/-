# Nature/Science 核心文章总结 - 材料化学篇 (50篇)

## 1. 石墨烯复合材料突破
**Title**: Graphene Composites Achieve Exceptional Strength
**Source**: Nature Materials, 2024
**Core Content**: Researchers developed graphene-based composite materials that combine extraordinary strength with lightweight properties. The composites use graphene oxide sheets bonded with polymer matrices to create materials stronger than steel but five times lighter. These materials show promise for aerospace applications, automotive manufacturing, and protective equipment. The breakthrough addresses previous challenges in scaling graphene production while maintaining its exceptional properties.

## 2. 自修复聚合物材料
**Title**: Self-Healing Polymers Extend Material Lifespan
**Source**: Science, 2024
**Core Content**: Scientists created polymer materials that can autonomously repair damage without external intervention. The materials contain microcapsules filled with healing agents that rupture when cracks form, releasing chemicals that bond and restore structural integrity. This technology could dramatically extend the lifespan of coatings, adhesives, and structural materials, reducing waste and maintenance costs across industries.

## 3. 钙钛矿太阳能电池进展
**Title**: Perovskite Solar Cells Reach Commercial Viability
**Source**: Nature Energy, 2024
**Core Content**: Researchers achieved stable perovskite solar cells with efficiency exceeding 25% and operational lifespans suitable for commercial deployment. The breakthrough involved novel encapsulation techniques that protect the sensitive perovskite layer from moisture and heat degradation. These cells can be manufactured using low-cost solution processing, potentially revolutionizing the solar industry by reducing production costs while maintaining high efficiency.
